CineHub

Stop losing movie plans in the group chat.

The problem

Groups of friends waste time going back and forth in WhatsApp trying to agree on which movie to watch, at what cinema, and at what time. Movie info gets buried in chat scroll, and there's no single place to propose a plan and see who's actually confirmed to come.

What CineHub does

  • Browse popular movies and see their details
  • Pick a showtime: cinema, date, and hour
  • Create a plan around that showtime
  • Share the plan with friends
  • Friends respond Yes / Maybe / No so everyone knows who's coming
